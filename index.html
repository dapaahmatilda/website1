Birth & Family

My name is Matilda Korlekuor Dapaah. I was born and raised in Accra, the capital city of Ghana, into a loving and supportive family. Growing up, my family taught me the values of respect, kindness, and hard work.

We have always shared strong family bonds, which have helped shape me into the person I am today. My parents have been a great source of motivation, constantly encouraging me to aim high and stay focused on my dreams.

Education

I began my basic education at Corpus Christi Catholic School, where I built a strong academic foundation and developed a love for learning. After completing my basic education, I continued to Methodist Girls’ High School in the Eastern Region of Ghana from 2021 to 2024.

During my time there, I worked hard, built lasting friendships, and took part in various school activities that helped me improve my communication and leadership skills.

I am currently based in Accra and enrolled in AI Career Essentials, where I am learning valuable digital and professional skills to prepare me for future opportunities.

Religion

I am a Christian, and my faith plays a major role in my daily life. I believe in honesty, compassion, and gratitude, and I always strive to live according to Christian values.

My religion has helped me remain humble and hopeful, even when facing challenges.

Social Life

Socially, I am a friendly and approachable person who enjoys meeting new people and learning from them. I love engaging in meaningful conversations, helping others, and participating in community and school activities.

I believe that building positive relationships with people from different backgrounds helps broaden one’s understanding of the world. I also enjoy spending time with my friends and family, which brings me joy and balance in life.

Thank you for reading my autobiography!
