1. Jollof Rice

Main Ingredients: Rice, Tomatoes, Tomato Paste, Onions, Pepper, Vegetable Oil, and Seasoning.

Preparation: Fry blended tomatoes, onions, and pepper in oil. Add tomato paste and seasoning. Stir and cook until the sauce thickens. Add the washed rice and water, then cook until the rice is soft and well mixed with the sauce.

2. Banku and Okro Soup

Main Ingredients: Corn Dough, Cassava Dough, Okro, Fish, Meat, Palm Oil, and Pepper.

Preparation: Mix corn dough and cassava dough with water and stir over low heat until thick and smooth. For the soup, fry onions and pepper in palm oil, add okro, fish, and meat, and cook until tender. Serve hot with banku.

3. Fried Plantain and Beans (Red-Red)

Main Ingredients: Ripe Plantain, Black-eyed Beans, Palm Oil, Onions, and Pepper.

Preparation: Boil the beans until soft, then fry blended pepper and onions in palm oil. Mix the sauce with the cooked beans. Slice and fry the ripe plantains until golden brown and serve with the beans stew.

4. Fufu with Light Soup

Main Ingredients: Cassava, Plantain, Tomatoes, Pepper, Onion, Fish, and Meat.

Preparation: Boil and pound cassava and plantain together until smooth to form fufu. For the soup, boil blended tomatoes, onions, and pepper with fish and meat, and cook until the soup thickens. Serve hot with fufu.

5. Waakye

Main Ingredients: Rice, Beans, Millet Leaves, Onions, Pepper, and Oil.

Preparation: Boil beans with millet leaves until half-cooked, then add rice and cook together until both are soft. Serve with stew, gari, and fried fish or egg.

These are my favorite meals that I enjoy most of the time. They are delicious and rich in nutrients.
